Stateflow chart for a DC fast-charging sequencer, generated by an AI agent.
✗ Without the rules
Transition labels rendered on top of each other — two guards and their action bodies occupy the same space. Each arc carries its full expression inline. Functionally it may be fine; nobody can review it.
✓ With the rules
Every guard computed once into a named flag, so each arc carries [bWeldTimeout] and nothing else. States in execution order, transition priorities visible, no overlap anywhere.
